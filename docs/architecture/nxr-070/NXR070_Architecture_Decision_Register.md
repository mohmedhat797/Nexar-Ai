# NXR-070 — Architecture Decision Register

Status legend: **Confirmed by owner** · **Conditionally accepted by owner** · **Recommended, pending** · **Requires further evidence** · **Deferred** · **Rejected**.

The owner confirmed two directions: multi-account priority (Decision A) and context continuity (Decision B). The five final corrections (C1–C5) are accepted by the owner as the basis for the proposed architecture, subject to clarifications K1–K2 ([closure report](NXR070_Final_Corrections_Closure_Report.md)). This acceptance does **not** approve ADR-029, and no other approval is inferred. ADR-029 remains **Proposed**.
